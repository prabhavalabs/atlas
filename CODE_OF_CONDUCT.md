# Community code of conduct

Atlas is built for public benefit. Contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation welcoming, safe, and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect across differences in background, identity, expertise, and language.
- Give specific, evidence-based feedback and assume good intent while addressing harmful impact.
- Respect disaster-affected communities and avoid sensationalizing suffering.
- Protect private, sensitive, embargoed, and personally identifying information.
- Accept maintainers' responsibility to prioritize public safety, licensing, accessibility, and operational sustainability.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate misinformation, credential disclosure, exploitative disaster imagery, and sustained disruption are not permitted in project spaces.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to repository activity and other spaces where a person represents Atlas or Prabhava Labs. Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting form and begin the report title with `Conduct report`. Maintainers will protect confidentiality where possible and may warn, restrict, remove, or ban participation according to the severity and pattern of behavior.
